**SKILL.md:**
- The primers are attached in the established style: surfacing-alternatives before step 0
  and the sample checkpoint; first-principles (Open and Challenge); oblique-strategies when stuck.
- Step 0 gains the purpose-first, scope, declared-frame, adjacent-route and held-out-family moves.
- The sample checkpoint is rewritten to the verified sequence:

  - a staged challenge (blind, push, compare, judge) with cues that differ from the author's;
  - a coverage statement led by the split signal, with observed counts only;
  - triggers matched to remedies;
