# Assess the research package boundary and implications for methodology review

## Brief and decision relevance

Andrew asks whether Research Pipeline and Agentic Research should merge because
they overlap and participate in one process. Decide whether a full merger, a
shared installation experience, or separate packages with clearer integration
would improve adoption and use enough to justify maintenance and compatibility
costs. Then assess where methodology-review fits and which research capabilities
it should reuse. This commissions investigation and recommendations, not package
changes, publication, corpus conversion or methodology-review implementation.

Andrew confirmed mixed scope and standard verification in this conversation.
The existing ownership boundaries are current constraints to evaluate explicitly,
not proof that the current packaging is optimal. Workbench is relevant upstream
context; adopting it is outside this assessment.

## Scope and shape

One bounded architecture decision, authored inline. Compare the same criteria
across separate packages, a coordinated installation/bundle, and a full merger:
dependency reality, user routing, host packaging support, update ownership,
compatibility, evidence integrity and simplification. Inspect current source and
official host documentation; do not infer usage outcomes from coherent prose.

Framing candidates considered: a whole-suite methods audit (too broad for the
current decision), separate host/engine/process campaigns (unnecessary coordination
for this bounded source set), and one package-boundary comparison (selected).
No specialist fan-out; standard verification includes one independent adversarial
pass and lead source/claim spot-checks. Prior composition records and skill rules
are lenses; implementation artifacts and fetched host documents supply observations.

## Acceptance

- Separate source-level merger, install experience and conceptual responsibility.
- Verify dependency direction and identify concrete costs of the existing seam.
- Present comparable options, disconfirming evidence and conditions that would
  change the recommendation.
- Explain methodology-review's placement and research-routing implications after
  the package comparison, preserving operator decisions and evidence ownership.
- Persist source attestations and analysis; pass citation checks, independent
  challenge and host spot-checks; regenerate knowledge discovery.

## Simplification opportunity

Identify redundant entrypoints or integration machinery without presupposing that
merging namespaces or copying the research engine is necessary.
