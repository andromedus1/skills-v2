Consider coverage of the canonical lint's warn-only JSON fields in consuming
summaries. `campaign_unbound` surfaces campaign-prefixed handles whose slug resolves
but whose campaign number the kernel cannot bind; `substrate_confidence_omitted`
is a lower-stakes deprecation notice. gate-citations and the AR verification-record
aid describe broken/thin/pattern results without explicitly describing these lists.
Preserve their warn-only policy and canonical ownership if this is taken up; no
kernel, schema, threshold or reporting design is selected here.

Origin: QVE root round 3, QVE/METH-I04, Claude job
`20261018T231314Z-e20e5283`. Driver verified the fields and reporting gap. Current
corpus: zero campaign_unbound handles and five substrate_confidence_omitted notices
(the assessor mistakenly said both lists were empty). Consequence is minor here:
no campaign-binding exposure is demonstrated, confidence omission is advisory,
and raw JSON remains available. Revisit with a multi-campaign corpus or when
improving advisory-summary completeness. This is distinct from the completed
version-number noise work, which preserved matching and improved grouping.
