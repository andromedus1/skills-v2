# Next methodology-review pilot: a different domain, bounded

Plan for the third real use of `research-pipeline:methodology-review`, after the
revision in `feature-methodology-review-codex-pilot-revision`. Two Knowledge runs
showed broader framing and honest limits, but only modest incremental decision
yield. The second run took 24 dispatches. One component, one reused corpus and two
driving models cannot show that the skill transfers or earns its effort. The next
test needs a domain other than Knowledge, and it needs less research to reuse.

This idea does not launch a pilot, select a component or authorize work in another
project. Scoping it requires Andrew to place the target project in scope.

## Target selection

Choose a component that meets all of these criteria:

- It is a domain other than knowledge management or retrieval, so the primer's
  Knowledge-adjacent examples cannot carry the result (P16).
- It has little prior research on its own methods, so proportional depth is tested
  without a large corpus to reuse.
- It has a real pending decision whose answer the operator would act on.
- Its owner can judge the packet's value at the end.

The default candidate is project-blueprint's data-science component. It is Andrew's
motivating case and the next step in blueprint-suite's
`idea-methodology-review-rollout`. Check the second criterion during scoping, without
inspecting it now. If its methods already have a large research corpus, prefer
another component that meets the criteria, or record the reuse as a known confound.

When the component is picked, add the run-2 line to blueprint-suite's
`idea-methodology-review-rollout`: Codex repeat on Knowledge, closed with retained
risks after 24 dispatches, followed by the skills-v2 revision from this arc.

## Charter to declare at kickoff

- **Jobs and outcomes:** what the component's methods are for, in the operator's
  words.
- **Decision:** one or two named choices the review may change, such as retaining,
  replacing or simplifying a method. With no decision, the pilot has nothing to
  measure.
- **Constraints:** fixed versus disputed, plus any operator exclusions.
- **Shape:** focused review unless a split clearly improves the decision.
- **Rounds:** default limit of five. A useful early stop is acceptable.

## Effort forecast (information, not a cap)

| Phase | Expected dispatches |
|---|---|
| Domain map (scout author, source verifier) | 2–3 |
| Staged completeness challenge | 3 |
| One decision-tied refresh and re-challenge, only if needed | 0–4 |
| Comparison (author, source review, correction if needed) | 2–4 |
| Full-scope assessment (step 4 and exit together) | 1 |
| **Total** | **about 8–15**, versus 24 on the Knowledge repeat |

Record actual counts beside this forecast at every checkpoint. Record tool-reported
usage only, and claim no total cost the tools do not report.

## Evidence that would change the decision

- **Supports the revision:** the review reaches comparison with recorded coverage
  limits and without unnecessary repeat challenges. A consequential refresh may still
  need one. Where one assessment is eligible on an unchanged baseline, the exit
  follows from it without a ritual question. The operator says the packet changed or
  substantiated a choice.
- **Against it:** breadth still loops before any decision. Decision-remote families
  get refreshed anyway. The operator finds the packet restates known limits. Effort
  exceeds the forecast with no matching yield.
- **Unresolved:** the result depends on host improvisation that the skill does not
  state.

## Evaluation

Use the eight purpose tests from the Knowledge pilot log: finds relevant
alternatives, questions the frame, compares like jobs honestly, produces useful
decisions, recognizes evidence limits, preserves operator judgment, earns its
effort, and transfers beyond the domain. Also check
`feature-methodology-review-codex-pilot-revision`'s forward scenarios FS1–FS7
against what actually happened.

Classify each problem as a skill gap, a host execution error, or an ARD-owned source
issue. Record Andrew's own value judgment of the final packet; the Knowledge repeat
lacked one. Compare with the Knowledge runs with caution, because the corpus,
baseline, driver and skill all differ.
