# Decomposing and coordinating a methodology review

Use this reference when selecting a split, delegating a component review, or
resuming a review with children. The main skill owns the common protocol, authority,
round limit and exit outcomes; this reference adds coordination at any scale.

## Choose boundaries

Compare an unsplit review with useful cuts by decision, scenario or component;
record why the chosen cut helps, what it excludes and what coupling remains. Impose
no fixed candidate quota and do not copy ARD's research decomposition. Create a
child only when its own comparison and assessment adds a distinct answer for the
parent; one decision may simply need a multi-facet research campaign. Coordinated
children share one adoption decision while each returns a scoped methodology
assessment; independent adoption marks a program, not every child. Keep mutually
dependent decisions together and declare dependencies between independently
assessable ones. Each shared question has one owner whose results others reference.

If the boundary cannot yet be chosen, state the uncertainty: ask the operator when
intent or constraints are missing, otherwise commission bounded discovery for the
specific evidence gap, never an exhaustive review to find the scope.

Reapply the selector within a child only if its scope shrinks and a new comparison
has decision value; children may differ in shape and research depth. Stop splitting
when questions are bounded, evidence settles them, further cuts add no distinct
decision, or agreed limits are reached. Keep uncovered questions in the record,
never labeled reviewed.

## Run a child review

Record children as in
[review-record.md](review-record.md#component-coverage-and-coordination). Named
sections in one controller suffice for a coordinated review; a program needing
independently scheduled review items creates them through the project's workflow
and links them from the root. Every child links back to root and parent so resume
restores the shared limit. Child review items, like the root, carry no `research` tag or
dials; research commissioning items stay separate from review controllers.

Pass this skill and reference to a child worker with:

- **Identity and purpose:** root/child IDs and record links, parent question,
  covered questions and expected contribution.
- **Charter:** baseline, scope/exclusions, outcomes, criteria/scenarios and
  inherited constraints.
- **Evidence:** relevant current artifacts and known gaps; source verification
  stays with Agentic Research.
- **Coordination:** dependencies, shared-question owner, root round/limit and
  allocated next checkpoint.
- **Authority:** actual operator decisions and authorized actions; questions still
  needing adjudication.
- **Return location:** existing record section/item and the consuming parent.

Delegate only independent work whose coordination is useful; an inline child uses
the same contract. A child runs the common protocol on its scope and may propose a
smaller cut within it, but cannot enlarge its charter, reset limits, authorize
changes or rewrite a sibling's decisions. Batch related operator questions through
the parent where useful.

Each child returns a dated [assessment report](review-record.md#assessment-report)
for its scope, with an existing exit outcome and reason, plus each finding's
evidence references, uncertainty, contrary evidence and implications for the parent
or siblings, unresolved evidence requests, actual decisions and authorized actions
kept separate from recommendations, remaining obligations and the next
dependency/checkpoint.

Before its assessment runs, a child returns a checkpoint with no outcome, its
outstanding steps and next action; an ordinary in-progress checkpoint is not
incomplete. IDs follow the [finding record](review-record.md#finding-record). An
assessment handoff is not a clean exit: a child with pending decisions or remedies
returns that status, and a completed research engagement alone changes neither the
child's nor the parent's methodological verdict.

## Integrate at the parent

Read children's actual assessments and evidence, not verdict labels. Check coverage
against the parent charter, deduplicate findings with retained IDs, expose
contradictory recommendations, and test common scenarios across child boundaries,
where individually sound methods can conflict or leave a gap.

Operational integration is not research synthesis. The parent records coverage,
decision dependencies, remedy state and the whole-scope assessment; new cross-source
synthesis goes through Agentic Research, with research-program coordination only
when separate engagements warrant it. Child analyses are lenses; conclusions must
trace to source attestations, and external claims raised in integration stay
provisional until verified.

The fresh parent assessor receives the charter, current baseline, child coverage
and assessments, verified research, common scenarios and exact operator decisions,
and must examine interactions, persistent/retained risks and new findings. One fresh
assessment may cover several levels only when its documented inputs and coverage
satisfy each; no duplicate pass is needed to match a diagram.

## Rounds, stopping and resume

The skill's [round rules](../SKILL.md#6-fresh-reassessment-and-exit) apply; the root
owns the limit and count. When selecting a program, check whether the limit fits its
expected independent change cycles; explain a material mismatch and request an
adjustment only when needed.

Record the in-progress root round and allocated child checkpoints before dispatch.
A program child may complete decision → authorized change → child-scope
reassessment on its own schedule, without waiting for siblings, as its one cycle
for that root round; coordinated children contribute to the parent's combined cycle.
No child starts another cycle within the same root round.

Do not wait for every child to be clean to close a round; missing coverage is never
claimed as assessed, and a checkpoint without the parent assessment completes no round. Carry
pending work into the next allocation without restarting it or granting extra
cycles. Research verification corrections follow the engine's own contract; no
nested remediation loop bypasses the root limit. A blocked child returns its exact
awaiting/incomplete outcome while independent authorized work continues; a pending
checkpoint never permits repeating unchanged child work.

Before another round, identify the changed evidence, authorized change or operator
decision that permits progress. Reuse unaffected child assessments whose baseline
and coverage remain valid; re-evaluate affected children and interactions. At the
limit, apply the skill's exit ranking and surface unfinished coverage.

On resume, read the root record, then the relevant authoritative child records,
and before dispatch restore the charter/baseline, chosen cut, shared-question ownership,
dependencies, round/checkpoints, findings, decisions and pending remedies,
preserving coverage and finding IDs when boundaries change. Do not repeat satisfied
research or adjudication without a named freshness/coverage gap or changed decision
context. Recover an archived child's full report through retention conventions; a
terminal work-item stage alone cannot substitute for it.
