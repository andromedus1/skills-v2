## Anchored details (abstract)
- **Objective:** "a study of two systematic mapping studies to evaluate the reliability of
  mapping studies and point out some challenges related to this type of study in software
  engineering."
- **Method:** "an in-depth case study of two published mapping studies on software product line
  testing."
- **Results:** "despite the fact that the two studies are addressing the same topic, there are
  quite a number of differences when it comes to papers included and in terms of
  classification of the papers included in the two mapping studies."
- **Conclusion:** "although mapping studies are important, their reliability cannot simply be
  taken for granted."

  further research; their content was not read.
