# Making processes reliable: A validated PubMed search strategy for identifying new or emerging technologies (Varela-Lema et al., 2012)

## Source structure and scope
L. Varela-Lema, J. Puñal-Riobóo, B. Casal Acción, A. Ruano-Ravina, M. López García. IJTAHC
28(4):452-459, 2012. Engagement depth: **abstract only** (Cambridge Core page, as summarised by
the fetch tool).

## Anchored details (abstract)
- Strategy combined "free text terms commonly used in article titles to denote innovation" with
  index terms for the fields of interest.
- Sensitivity defined as "the proportion of relevant new or emerging technologies published in
  key journals that would be identified in the search strategy within the first 2 years of
  publication."
- Reference standard: six preselected key research journals and eight high-impact surgery
  journals; hand review of 12,061 journal abstracts identified 35 relevant technologies, 29 of
  which the search found within the 2-year window — 83% sensitivity.
- The search retrieved 6,228 abstracts, of which 459 were classified as new or emerging
  technologies.
