# Workbench design review priorities

## Anchored passages

- "Design review asks whether this is the best justified solution to the
  right problem—not merely whether the proposed design could work."
  "Correctness is a necessary baseline, but checking it alone is the least of
  the review's contribution."
- Priorities: "Right problem", "Best-fit solution", "Important omissions",
  "Repository fit and reuse", "Correctness baseline".
- "A finding need not demonstrate a correctness defect to be useful or
  material." "Do not turn alternatives into new requirements or restart
  settled choices without reason. Apply the project's overbuilding
  calibration".
- "A design or formal-model review of a concurrency protocol stops after its
  second round unless the remaining failures are hangs, crashes, or data
  corruption."
