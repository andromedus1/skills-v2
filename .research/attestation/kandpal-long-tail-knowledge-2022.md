# Large Language Models Struggle to Learn Long-Tail Knowledge

## Source structure and scope
Nikhil Kandpal, Haikang Deng, Adam Roberts, Eric Wallace, Colin Raffel; arXiv 2211.08411 v2 (2023-07-27);
ICML 2023. Abstract only read.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract:** a model's "ability to answer a fact-based question relates to how many documents associated with
  that question were seen during pre-training"; "strong correlational and causal relationships between accuracy
  and relevant document count"; larger models learn more long-tail knowledge but would need scaling "by many
  orders of magnitude" for competitive QA on questions with little pre-training support; "retrieval-augmentation
  can reduce the dependence on relevant pre-training information".
- Scope: fact-based QA, not enumeration of approaches.
