# Measuring Lexical Diversity of Synthetic Data Generated through Fine-Grained Persona Prompting

## Source structure and scope
Gauri Kambhatla, Chantal Shaib, Venkata Govindarajan; arXiv 2505.17390 v2 (2025-09-19); EMNLP Findings 2025.
Abstract only read.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract:** persona-driven synthetic prompts/instructions "are significantly less diverse than human-written ones";
  "persona prompting produces higher lexical diversity than prompting without personas, particularly in larger
  models"; "adding fine-grained persona details yields minimal gains in diversity compared to simply specifying a
  length cutoff in the prompt". Measures are lexical diversity and redundancy metrics.
