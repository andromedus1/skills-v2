# Evaluation criteria to assess the value of identification sources for horizon scanning (Smith, Cook, Packer, 2010)

## Anchored details (abstract)
- **Objectives:** the English National Horizon Scanning Centre routinely examined thirty-five
  sources to identify emerging health technologies; the study created evaluation criteria and a
  cutoff score for removing under-performing sources.
- **Methods:** criteria developed from literature review and analyst discussion; piloted on six
  sources; applied to all 35 using the Analytic Hierarchy Process.
- **Results:** eight criteria judged most relevant; the three primary criteria were coverage,
  quality and efficiency; seven sources fell below the threshold and were recommended for
  discontinuation.
- **Conclusions:** framework useful for existing and new sources; other organisations could adapt
  and validate the criteria.
