# Systematic review identifies six metrics and one method for assessing literature search effectiveness but no consensus on appropriate use

## Source structure and scope
Cooper C, Varley-Campbell J, Booth A, Britten N, Garside R. Journal of Clinical Epidemiology
2018 (DOI 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2018.02.025). Systematic review. Engagement depth: abstract only
(no open full text located).

## Anchored details (abstract)
- **Results:** 50 studies included; "Six metrics (sensitivity, specificity, precision, accuracy,
  number needed to read, and yield) and one method (capture recapture) were identified."
- **Title claim:** "no consensus on appropriate use".
- **Conclusion:** effectiveness studies "need to identify clearly the threshold at which they
  will define effectiveness"; reporting of confidence intervals advised; search methods used
  to derive estimates "should be clearly reported and validated".
