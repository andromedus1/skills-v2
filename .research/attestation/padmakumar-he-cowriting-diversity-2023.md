# Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?

## Source structure and scope
Vishakh Padmakumar, He He; arXiv 2309.05196 v3 (2024-07-01); ICLR 2024. Abstract only read.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract, design:** argumentative essays written with a base LLM (GPT3), a feedback-tuned LLM
  (InstructGPT), or without help; a set of diversity metrics developed.
- **Abstract, results:** "writing with InstructGPT (but not the GPT3) results in a statistically significant
  reduction in diversity"; it increases similarity between different authors' writing and reduces lexical
  and content diversity; the effect is "mainly attributable to InstructGPT contributing less diverse text";
  user-contributed text is unaffected. Authors suggest quality gains from human-feedback adaptation "might
  come at the cost of more homogeneous and less diverse content."
