# Workbench simplification posture reference

## Anchored passages

- Resolution order: "explicit user direction, `.work/CONVENTIONS.md`, then
  `balanced`".
- `hygiene`: "Do not broaden the boundary to hunt for refactors."
  `balanced`: "actively seek cohesive simplification across the affected
  contract boundary". `structural`: "challenge the decomposition of the full
  authorized outcome boundary".
- "Every posture preserves observable behavior" and "measured performance
  constraints".
- "Simplicity means fewer durable concepts, branches, layers, options,
  compatibility paths, and lower operating and verification cost—not merely
  fewer lines or a smaller diff. A refactor must have a stated payoff".
- "The posture does not expand the user's authorized outcome"; unrelated
  simplification is "a non-blocking follow-up" to park.
- Applied by phase: design, implementation, review.
