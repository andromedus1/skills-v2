# Recognition-Primed Decision Model (Gary Klein, author website page)

## Source structure and scope
Short overview page on the originator's own website (gary-klein.com, "RPD"), undated; read in full. A
summary statement of the model, not a study report; no data are presented.

## Anchored details
- **Page text:** "In 1985, Gary and colleagues Roberta Calderwood and Anne Clinton-Cirocco developed the
  Recognition-Primed Decision (RPD) model to explain how experienced fireground commanders could use their
  expertise to identify and carry out a course of action without having to generate and analyze a set of
  options."
- **Page text:** "The most critical assertion of the RPD model is that people can use experience to
  generate a plausible option as the first one they consider." The model "stemmed from Naturalistic
  Decision Making research."
