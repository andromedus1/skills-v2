# How much searching is enough? Comprehensive versus optimal retrieval for technology assessments

## Source structure and scope
Booth A. International Journal of Technology Assessment in Health Care 2010 (DOI
10.1017/S0266462310000966; volume 26(4): 431-435 as listed in the bibliography of
cooper-search-process-guidance-2018). Methodological review. Engagement depth: abstract only
(publisher page not fetched in full).

## Anchored details (abstract)
- **Methods:** reviews methodology literature "for evaluating yield from literature searching
  strategies and for deciding whether to continue or desist in the searching process."
- **Results:** "Eight different methods were identified": Capture-recapture; Feedback from the
  commissioner; seeking the Disconfirming case; comparison against a known Gold standard;
  evaluating retrieval of Known items; the Law of diminishing returns; a priori Stopping rules;
  identifying a point of Theoretical saturation.
- **Conclusions:** "there has been very little formal evaluation of the specific strengths and
  weaknesses of the different techniques"; proposes an evaluation agenda including "the
  specific impact of missing relevant studies."
