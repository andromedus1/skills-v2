# Capture-mark-recapture to estimate the number of missed articles for systematic reviews in surgery

## Source structure and scope
Stelfox HT, Foster G, Niven D, Kirkpatrick AW, Goldsmith CH. American Journal of Surgery 2013
(DOI 10.1016/j.amjsurg.2012.11.017). Application to one systematic review of adult trauma care
quality indicators. Engagement depth: structured abstract.

## Anchored details (abstract)
- 40 articles included "from online searches and citation references".
- "The CMR model suggested that 3 (95% confidence interval [CI]: 0 to 6) articles were missed and
  the database search provided 93% (one-sided 95% CI: >=83%) of known articles".
- "The search order used for identifying the articles was optimal among the 24 that could have
  been used."
- Conclusion: CMR "can be used ... to estimate the closeness to capturing the total body of
  literature for a specific topic."
