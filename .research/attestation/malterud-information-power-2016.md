# Sample Size in Qualitative Interview Studies: Guided by Information Power

## Source structure and scope
Malterud K, Siersma VD, Guassora AD. Qualitative Health Research 2016 (DOI
10.1177/1049732315617444). Conceptual model. Engagement depth: abstract.

## Anchored details (abstract)
- "The prevailing concept for sample size in qualitative studies is 'saturation.' Saturation is
  closely tied to a specific methodology, and the term is inconsistently applied."
- Proposes "information power": "the more information the sample holds, relevant for the actual
  study, the lower amount of participants is needed."
- Sample adequacy depends on "(a) the aim of the study, (b) sample specificity, (c) use of
  established theory, (d) quality of dialogue, and (e) analysis strategy."
- Model intended for use in planning and during data collection; no empirical validation is
  reported in the abstract.
