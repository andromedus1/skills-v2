# Guidelines for conducting systematic mapping studies in software engineering: An update (Petersen, Vakkalanka, Kuzniarz, 2015)

## Source structure and scope
Kai Petersen, Sairam Vakkalanka, Ludwik Kuzniarz. Information & Software Technology 65:1-18,
2015 (Durham EBSE bibliography page). Engagement depth: **abstract only**; full text is not open
access (Semantic Scholar lists status CLOSED).

## Anchored details (abstract)
- **Context:** "Systematic mapping studies are used to structure a research area, while
  systematic reviews are focused on gathering and synthesizing evidence"; the prior mapping
  guidelines dated from 2008.
- **Method:** "a systematic mapping study of systematic maps, considering some practices of
  systematic review guidelines as well."
- **Results:** "In a large number of studies multiple guidelines are used and combined, which
  leads to different ways in conducting mapping studies."
- **Conclusion:** "The most frequently followed guidelines are not sufficient alone"; updated
  guidelines consolidating findings were proposed.
