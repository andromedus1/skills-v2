## Anchored details
- **Summary page:** five experiments; ChatGPT-assisted brainstorming produced narrower idea sets; the
  drop in diversity was significant "in 37 of 45" statistical comparisons.
- **Toy example (brick and fan):** 94% of ChatGPT-assisted ideas shared overlapping concepts; nine
  participants independently named their toy "Build-a-Breeze Castle"; human-generated ideas were
  described as entirely unique.

  voices repeating similar thoughts."
