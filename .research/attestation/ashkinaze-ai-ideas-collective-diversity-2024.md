# How AI Ideas Affect the Creativity, Diversity, and Evolution of Human Ideas: Evidence From a Large, Dynamic Experiment

## Source structure and scope
Joshua Ashkinaze, Julia Mendelsohn, Li Qiwei, Ceren Budak, Eric Gilbert; arXiv 2401.13481 v3
(2025-07-31); ACM Collective Intelligence 2025. Abstract only read; metrics not audited.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract, design:** 800+ participants, 40+ countries, viewed example ideas from ChatGPT or prior
  participants, then brainstormed; AI exposure none/low/high; disclosure labelling varied; dynamic design
  in which prior participants' ideas become stimuli for later participants ("LLMs 'in the culture loop'").
- **Abstract, results:** "high AI exposure (but not low AI exposure) did not affect the creativity of
  individual ideas but did increase the average amount and rate of change of collective idea diversity.
  AI made ideas different, not better." No main effect of disclosure. "introducing AI ideas may increase
  collective diversity but not individual creativity."
