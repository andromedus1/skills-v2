# Blind Spots in Industry and Competitor Analysis: The Implications of Interfirm (Mis)perceptions for Strategic Decisions (Zajac and Bazerman, 1991)

## Source structure and scope
Edward J. Zajac and Max Bazerman; Academy of Management Review, 1991 (the Kellogg research
page lists pages "37-56(1)"). Engagement depth: **abstract-level summary** on the Kellogg
School research page; the article's typology of blind spots was not read.

## Anchored details (abstract page)
- "decision makers typically have specific 'blind spots' when they consider the contingent
  decisions of competitors."
- The article bridges competitor analysis and behavioural strategic decision-making literatures.
- These blind spots are offered as explanations for recurring outcomes: industry overcapacity,
  new business entry failures, and acquisition premiums.
- Conceptual argument; the page reports no measured prevalence of the blind spots.
