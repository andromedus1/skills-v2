# Standardizing the Measurement of Text Diversity: A Tool and a Comparative Analysis of Scores

## Source structure and scope
Chantal Shaib, Venkata S. Govindarajan, Joe Barrow, Jiuding Sun, Alexa F. Siu, Byron C. Wallace, Ani Nenkova;
arXiv 2403.00553 v3 (2026-02-18); AACL 2025. Abstract only read.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract:** "there is no standard method to measure" diversity of LLM outputs; compression-based algorithms
  capture similar information to n-gram overlap; recommends reporting a combination — compression ratios, long
  n-gram self-repetition, Self-BLEU and BERTScore — because of their low mutual correlation; releases the
  `diversity` Python package.
