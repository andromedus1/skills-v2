# The current state of scenario development: an overview of techniques (Bishop, Hines, Collins, 2007)

## Source structure and scope
Peter Bishop, Andy Hines, Terry Collins. Foresight, vol. 9 issue 1, pp. 5-25, published
27 February 2007, Emerald. Engagement depth: **structured abstract only** (publisher page); the
technique-by-technique evaluations were not read. A previously listed open PDF mirror
(samiconsulting.co.uk) returned HTTP 404.

## Anchored details (structured abstract)
- **Purpose:** "review all the techniques for developing scenarios that have appeared in the
  literature, along with comments on their utility, strengths and weaknesses."
- **Approach:** "an electronic search using internet search engines and online databases and
  indexes."
- **Findings:** "eight categories of techniques that include a total of 23 variations used to
  develop scenarios. There are descriptions and evaluations for each."
- **Practical implications:** lets futurists "broaden their repertoire of scenario techniques."
- **Originality:** "scenario development is the stock-in-trade of futures studies" yet no
  comprehensive catalogue existed; the work is "the start at developing a consensus list of
  techniques that can be refined as the field matures."
- Per the web-search summary of the same paper (not the publisher page), the eight categories
  are judgment, baseline, elaboration of fixed scenarios, event sequences, backcasting,
  dimensions of uncertainty, cross-impact analysis, and systems modelling ({confidence:
  search-summary}).
