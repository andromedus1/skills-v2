# What is an adequate sample size? Operationalising data saturation for theory-based interview studies

## Source structure and scope
Francis JJ, Johnston M, Robertson C, Glidewell L, Entwistle V, Eccles MP, Grimshaw JM. Psychology &
Health 2010 (DOI 10.1080/08870440903194015). Methods proposal with two demonstration studies.
Engagement depth: abstract.

## Anchored details (abstract)
- "there is no agreed method of establishing" data saturation.
- Principles for theory-based studies "where conceptual categories are pre-established by
  existing theory": specify "a minimum sample size for initial analysis (initial analysis
  sample)" and "how many more interviews will be conducted without new ideas emerging (stopping
  criterion)"; demonstrated with initial sample 10 and stopping criterion 3.
- Study 1 (retrospective, 14 GPs, 84 shared beliefs): "The criterion for saturation was achieved
  for Normative beliefs but not for other beliefs or studywise saturation." Study 2 (prospective,
  17 relatives, 44 beliefs): studywise saturation at interview 17.
- Proposes these principles for reporting; "may be adaptable for other types of studies."
