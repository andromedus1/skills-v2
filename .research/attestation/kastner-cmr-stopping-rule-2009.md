# The capture-mark-recapture technique can be used as a stopping rule when searching in systematic reviews

## Source structure and scope
Kastner M, Straus SE, McKibbon KA, Goldsmith CH. Journal of Clinical Epidemiology 2009 (DOI
10.1016/j.jclinepi.2008.06.001; 62: 149-157 per the Cochrane Handbook chapter 4 bibliography).
Retrospective evaluation. Engagement depth: structured abstract.

## Anchored details (abstract)
- **Objective:** "Researchers have no empirically based search stopping rule"; tests a stopping
  strategy based on CMR ("the Horizon Estimate") across Medline, EMBASE, CINAHL, EBM reviews.
- **Results:** CMR suggests 1,838 potential articles at title/abstract level (1,246 found) and
  49 at full-text level (42 found); "The four databases provided 68% of known articles for the
  first level of screening and 81% for full-text screening."
- **Conclusions:** CMR "can be used ... to estimate the closeness to capturing the total body of
  literature"; "More studies are needed to objectively determine the usefulness of Horizon
  Estimates as a stopping rule strategy".
