# Routine development of objectively derived search strategies

## Source structure and scope
Hausner E, Waffenschmidt S, Kaiser T, Simon M. Systematic Reviews 2012; 1: 19 (DOI
10.1186/2046-4053-1-19, PMC3351720). Methods description from IQWiG with a worked example.
Engagement depth: full text (abstract, methods, example).

## Anchored details
- **Four steps:** "(1) generation of a test set, (2) development of the search strategy
  (objectively derived approach), (3) validation of the search strategy and (4) standardized
  documentation."
- **Test set:** references included in existing comprehensive systematic reviews are treated as
  a "quasi-gold standard"; they are "split randomly, using two-thirds for the development
  (development set) and one-third for the validation (validation set)".
- **Term derivation:** text-mining frequency analysis of the development set against a
  population set (terms present in at least 20% of development-set references examined).
- **Validation:** "To confirm that the strategy developed works with a different set of
  references, the strategy is tested against a validation set" containing different
  references than the development set.
- **Worked example:** 38 references from 3 systematic reviews; 25 development, 13 validation;
  the strategy captured all development references and "The validation set was also completely
  identified."
