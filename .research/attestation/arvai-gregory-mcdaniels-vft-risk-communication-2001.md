# Testing a Structured Decision Approach: Value-Focused Thinking for Deliberative Risk Communication (Arvai, Gregory & McDaniels 2001)

## Source structure and scope
Joseph L. Arvai, Robin Gregory, Timothy L. McDaniels. Risk Analysis 21(6), 2001, pp. 1065–1076 (RePEc
record). Engagement depth: abstract only; full text not read, so measures and effect sizes are not
audited.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract (design):** "six groups comprised of 7 to 10 people participated in conventional
  'alternative-focused' risk communication workshops and eight groups participated in similar
  'value-focused' workshops," all on managing risks to riverine salmon habitat from hydroelectric
  generation.
- **Abstract (hypotheses):** value-focused structure would lead to "more thoughtful, better informed, and
  hence higher quality decisions by helping them to consider and discuss a wider array of decision-relevant
  issues," and to greater comfort and satisfaction.
- **Abstract (result):** "The results provided support for the hypotheses: the value-focused decision
  structure led to more thoughtful and better informed risk management decisions."
