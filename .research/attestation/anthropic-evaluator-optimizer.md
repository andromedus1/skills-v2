# Building effective agents

## Source structure and scope
Engineering guidance, published 2024-12-19; fetched page warns that tooling has
changed. The cited pattern sections remain visible. This is guidance, not a
controlled evaluation of a particular review process.

## Anchored details
- **Workflow: Evaluator-optimizer:** separate generation and evaluation calls;
  suitability depends on clear criteria and demonstrable refinement value.
- **Agents:** environmental results supply feedback; iteration limits bound work.
- **When (and when not) to use agents:** added complexity trades time and cost
  against performance; simpler approaches can suffice.
