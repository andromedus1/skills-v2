# PRISMA-S: an extension to the PRISMA Statement for Reporting Literature Searches in Systematic Reviews

## Source structure and scope
Rethlefsen ML, Kirtley S, Waffenschmidt S, Ayala AP, Moher D, Page MJ, Koffel JB, and the
PRISMA-S Group. Systematic Reviews 2021; 10: article 39; DOI 10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z
(PMC7839230), as printed in the full-text record. Reporting guideline with explanation and
elaboration. Engagement depth: full text (abstract, methods, checklist table, item-by-item
elaboration, discussion).

## Anchored details
- **Abstract / Methods:** developed via a 3-stage Delphi survey, consensus conference and
  public review; 405 potential items initially identified; final checklist has 16 items.
- **Stated intent (abstract, Objective):** a checklist "to verify that each component of a
  search is completely reported and therefore reproducible." It is a reporting guideline; its
  completeness criterion is completeness of *reporting*, not of the retrieved set.
- **Checklist (Table 1), 16 items:** 1 Database name; 2 Multi-database searching; 3 Study
  registries; 4 Online resources and browsing; 5 Citation searching ("Indicate whether cited
  references or citing references were examined, and describe any methods used..."); 6 Contacts
  (authors, experts, manufacturers, others); 7 Other methods; 8 Full search strategies ("copied
  and pasted exactly as run"); 9 Limits and restrictions; 10 Search filters; 11 Prior work; 12
  Updates; 13 Dates of searches; 14 Peer review ("Describe any search peer review process");
  15 Total records (per database and source); 16 Deduplication.
- **Item 5 elaboration:** citation searching may go "beyond one level forwards and backwards"
  and authors "must cite the 'base' article(s)" on which citation searching was performed.
- **Item 7 elaboration:** many supplementary methods (personal files, "Related Articles"
  features) "may not be reproducible"; "it is still important to declare that the method was
  used, even if it may not be fully replicable."
- **Item 14 elaboration:** peer review of searches recommended via PRESS; benefits stated as
  "reduction of errors" and that it "may also increase the number of relevant records found".
- **Item 15 elaboration:** per-source record counts let readers see "whether databases or
  expert contacts constituted the majority of the records reviewed".
- **Web searching (Item 4):** teams may "pre-designate a limit to the number of results they
  review"; if so, this "should be noted in the text, along with the rationale."
- **Absence check (whole text):** the strings "saturat" and "stop" do not occur; the
  checklist contains no item for estimating or reporting how complete the retrieved set is
  (no stopping rule, no capture-recapture, no recall estimate).
- **Discussion, scope:** PRISMA-S "does not address using text mining or text analysis
  methods to create the search" and "does not require that decisions about the rationale
  behind choices in search terms and search construction be recorded".
