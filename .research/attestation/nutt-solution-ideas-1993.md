# The Identification of Solution Ideas During Organizational Decision Making (Nutt 1993)

## Source structure and scope
Paul C. Nutt. Management Science 39(9), 1993, pp. 1071–1085 (RePEc record). Engagement depth: abstract only;
full text not read.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract:** "The tactics that decision makers apply to identify ideas were uncovered from a systematic
  study of 168 decision cases." Success "measured by decision merit, development time, initial adoption, and
  sustained adoption."
- **Abstract:** "A 'synthesized template' which integrated useful practices and procedures from several
  sources had the most success, but this tactic was seldom used. A 'cyclical search' in which repeated
  searches were carried out to learn about opportunities was quite successful when used under conditions of
  low importance, low urgency, and good staff support. The success of the design tactic, which calls for
  innovation, improved when urgency was present and multiple alternatives were sought. The idea tactic
  which imposed a fully developed solution was widely used and seldom successful."
