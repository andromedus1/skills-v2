# Base Models Beat Aligned Models at Randomness and Creativity

## Source structure and scope
Peter West, Christopher Potts; arXiv 2505.00047 v2 (2025-09-13), preprint. Abstract only read.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract:** on tasks requiring unpredictable outputs (random number generation, rock-paper-scissors and
  hide-and-seek, creative writing) base models "consistently outperform their popular aligned forms"; aligned models
  "tend towards narrow behaviors", e.g. "preferring to generate '7' over other uniformly random numbers", becoming
  "almost fully predictable in some game states", or "prioritizing pleasant writing over creative originality".
- "better performance on common benchmarks tends to correlate with worse performance on our tasks".
