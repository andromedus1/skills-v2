# Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback

## Source structure and scope
Aman Madaan and coauthors; arXiv version 2, revised 2023-05-25. Engagement depth:
abstract and bibliographic metadata only; experiment details were not audited.

## Anchored details
- **Abstract, sentences describing the approach and evaluation:** one model generates, critiques and refines
  outputs without extra training; evaluation spans seven tasks, from dialogue response generation to mathematical
  reasoning, and several models.
- **Abstract, closing sentences:** authors report preference/performance gains over
  one-step generation under their evaluated conditions.
- Those reported gains do not establish correctness of every revision or transfer
  to an untested model, task, prompt or review process.
