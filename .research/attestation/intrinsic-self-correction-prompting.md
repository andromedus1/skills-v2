## Anchored details

  using theoretical analysis and experiments.
- **Abstract, closing sentences:** authors identify zero temperature and fair prompts
  as factors enabling demonstrated self-correction across multiple models.
- This is a conditioned positive claim, not a universal guarantee for arbitrary
